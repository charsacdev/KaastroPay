Privacy policy

Effective 1 September 2026 Version 2.1 NG · GH · KE · TZ · UG
Prototype notice. This is a demonstration build. No real personal data is collected, and this document is illustrative rather than a reviewed legal instrument.

1. The short version

We collect what we need to run a regulated financial service and nothing beyond it. We do not sell your data. We do not use your information for advertising. The biggest single reason we hold anything is that the law requires us to know who our customers are.

2. What we collect

You give us

  • Name, date of birth, email address and phone number.
  • Your country of residence and residential address.
  • Your national identifier — NIN or BVN in Nigeria, Ghana Card in Ghana, national ID in Kenya, NIDA in Tanzania, national ID in Uganda.
  • A photo of an identity document, and a selfie taken at verification.
  • Bank account or mobile money details for payouts.
  • Anything you write to support.

We generate

  • Your transaction history, including amounts, assets, rates applied and references.
  • Deposit addresses issued to you, and destination addresses you save.
  • Login and device records: IP address, device type, and session times.
  • Risk and monitoring signals derived from your activity.

We do not collect

  • Your social media, contacts, photos or location beyond country-level.
  • Advertising identifiers. There is no ad tracking on this platform.
  • Your bank login credentials. We never ask for them and you should never give them to anyone.

3. Why we hold it

  • To provide the service — issue your account, execute trades, send payouts. Without this data there is no product.
  • To meet legal obligations — identity verification, sanctions screening, transaction monitoring and regulatory reporting are required of us, not optional.
  • To prevent fraud — detecting account takeover, structuring and stolen-funds patterns protects you as much as us.
  • To support you — answering a ticket about a transaction means being able to see that transaction.
  • To improve the product — aggregate, non-identifying usage patterns only.

4. Who we share it with

Only where it is necessary, and only what is necessary:

  • Identity verification providers, to confirm your national identifier against the issuing database.
  • Payment and payout providers, to move cash to your bank or mobile money account.
  • Custody and blockchain infrastructure providers, to hold and move crypto assets.
  • Sanctions and screening services, as part of the checks we are required to run.
  • Regulators and law enforcement, where we are legally compelled. We do not volunteer your data.

We never sell your personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers, data brokers or affiliates for marketing.

5. How long we keep it

  • Identity and verification records — at least 5 years after your account closes, as anti-money-laundering law requires.
  • Transaction records — at least 5 years, for the same reason.
  • Support conversations — 3 years.
  • Login and device logs — 12 months.

Closing your account does not immediately delete your records, because we are not permitted to delete them. It does stop us processing them for anything other than the legal obligation.

6. How we protect it

  • Encrypted in transit and at rest. Identity documents are held in a separate encrypted store with tighter access.
  • Staff access is role-based and logged. An agent sees only what their queue requires.
  • Every staff action against a customer account is written to an append-only audit log.
  • Two-factor authentication is mandatory for all staff.
  • We never store your card PAN. Where card details exist, they are rendered directly by the issuer to your device.

7. Your rights

Depending on your country, you can ask us to:

  • Show you the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct anything inaccurate.
  • Delete data we are not legally required to retain.
  • Export your data in a portable format.
  • Object to processing that is not required for the service or by law.
  • Withdraw consent for optional communications at any time.

Email privacy@kaastropay.com. We respond within 30 days. We will ask you to verify your identity first — otherwise a data request becomes an attack.

8. Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage for things the app needs to work: your session, your theme preference, and your selected country. There are no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Analytics, where used, is aggregate and does not identify you.

9. Where your data lives

Data is processed in the region closest to your market, and may be transferred between our processors where they operate elsewhere. Where a transfer leaves your country, we require contractual protections equivalent to the ones your law provides.

10. Children

The platform is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and we close any account we find belongs to a minor.

11. Contact

You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your own country.
