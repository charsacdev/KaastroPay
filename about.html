Our story

Three problems we could not stop complaining about

Everyone who trades crypto in West and East Africa has the same three complaints, and they have nothing to do with crypto.

Nobody would tell you the rate. You asked in a group chat. Someone quoted you a number. Someone else got a different number. If you were new, you got the worse one, and there was no way to know.

Everything happened on somebody else's schedule. Your vendor was asleep, or travelling, or had "network issues". A sale that should take two minutes took until tomorrow, and there was nothing you could do about it.

When it went wrong, nobody owned it. No reference number, no receipt, no record of what rate was applied. Just a screenshot and an argument.

We built Kaastro Pay to fix those three things specifically. Publish one rate. Settle automatically, around the clock. Put a reference and a receipt on everything, including the mistakes.
