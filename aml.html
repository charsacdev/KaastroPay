1. Our commitment

A crypto-to-cash platform is exactly the kind of business criminals would like to use. We take that seriously, and we would rather lose a customer than take money we cannot account for.

Our programme covers customer identification, sanctions screening, ongoing transaction monitoring, record keeping, reporting, and staff training. A named compliance officer owns it and reports to the board.

2. Know Your Customer

Every user is identity-verified before they can move money. There is no anonymous tier and no way to trade without completing it.

3. Screening

  • Sanctions — every user is screened at onboarding and rescreened whenever lists are updated.
  • Politically exposed persons — matches trigger enhanced due diligence and senior sign-off before the account can trade.
  • Adverse media — checked at onboarding and on escalation.
  • Blockchain analytics — deposit addresses are screened against known illicit clusters, mixers and sanctioned addresses.

A positive sanctions match freezes the account immediately. We do not release those funds without regulatory direction.

4. Transaction monitoring

Monitoring runs continuously against the transaction stream, not in a nightly batch. Patterns we alert on include:

  • Structuring — repeated amounts sitting just under a reporting or tier threshold.
  • Rapid in-and-out — funds deposited and withdrawn with no genuine trading, which is what a pass-through account looks like.
  • Velocity anomalies — activity that jumps sharply against the account's own history.
  • Counterparty clustering — many unrelated users paying out to one destination.
  • Tainted deposits — funds arriving from an address linked to theft, ransomware or a sanctioned entity.
  • Third-party funding — cash arriving from an account that is not in the customer's name.

Alerts go to a human. Where the concern is material we suspend the account and hold the funds while we investigate.

5. Manual intervention controls

When a provider fails, an agent can confirm a deposit or payout by hand so customers are not left stranded. That capability is also the most obvious internal fraud risk in a business like ours, so it is tightly controlled:

  • A documented reason and written notes are mandatory.
  • Supporting evidence must be attached — a statement, an explorer record, or provider correspondence.
  • Amounts above a set threshold require a second approver.
  • Each agent has a daily cap on manual actions.
  • Every action is permanently attributed and reviewed weekly by an administrator, who watches each agent's manual-to-automatic ratio.

6. Reporting and record keeping

We file suspicious transaction reports with the financial intelligence unit in the relevant jurisdiction, within the statutory deadline. We do not tip off the subject of a report — if your account is restricted for this reason we may be legally barred from telling you why.

Identity records, transaction records and the reasoning behind decisions are retained for at least five years after an account closes.

7. What we will not process

  • Funds from darknet marketplaces, ransomware, or known theft.
  • Transactions involving sanctioned individuals, entities or jurisdictions.
  • Mixer or tumbler output where the source cannot be established.
  • Deposits from a third party on a customer's behalf.
  • Activity consistent with unlicensed money transmission or running an exchange on top of ours.

8. Staff and governance

Every employee completes AML training at induction and annually. Agents get additional training on their specific escalation duties. The programme is independently reviewed each year, and findings go to the board.

9. Raising a concern

If you believe an account is being used for fraud or money laundering, or you have found a security vulnerability, tell us:

  • compliance@kaastropay.com — suspicious activity, law enforcement requests, regulatory correspondence.
  • security@kaastropay.com — vulnerability disclosure. Report privately first and we will credit you.

Reports can be made anonymously. We do not retaliate against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.
