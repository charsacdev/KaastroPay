Your country is set at signup and determines your base currency, your identity check, and how you fund and withdraw. It cannot be changed afterwards without closing the account and opening a new one.

Verification tiers

Your limits depend on how far you have verified. Tier 0 cannot move money at all. Higher tiers require progressively more documentation. We may ask for source-of-funds evidence at any tier where a pattern warrants it, and we may hold a transaction until we receive it.

4. Deposits, trading and withdrawals

Crypto deposits

Deposit addresses are issued per user, per asset, per network. Sending a different asset, or the correct asset on a different network, may make the funds unrecoverable. Where recovery is technically possible we will attempt it as a manual case, may charge a recovery fee, and cannot guarantee success.

Deposits credit after the published number of network confirmations. Deposits below the published minimum are held rather than credited and require an agent to release.

Trading

Buy, sell and swap prices are quoted with our spread included. When you confirm a trade we issue a quote that pins the rate for a short window, shown on screen. If the quote expires before you confirm, you are re-quoted at the then-current price. The rate recorded on your receipt is the rate that was applied — not a later reconstruction.

We may suspend quoting entirely if our price feeds disagree beyond tolerance or a liquidity provider is unavailable. We would rather stop than price you incorrectly.

Withdrawals

Cash payouts go only to an account in your own name. Crypto withdrawals go only to addresses you have saved, and new destinations are subject to a 24-hour cooling period.

On-chain transactions are irreversible. Once broadcast we cannot recall them. You are responsible for the accuracy of the destination address and network you provide.

5. Fees

Current fees are always shown before you confirm a transaction. In summary:

  • Deposits — no fee from us. Cash deposits may carry your provider's own charge.
  • Trading — our margin is built into the quoted rate and disclosed on the rates screen.
  • Cash withdrawals — a flat fee per payout, published per country.
  • Crypto withdrawals — the network fee, passed through at cost.

We do not charge account, inactivity or closure fees.

We are a custodial service: we hold assets on your behalf. We maintain reserves against customer balances and reconcile them daily, but you should understand that a custodial balance is a claim on us rather than direct control of the underlying asset.
