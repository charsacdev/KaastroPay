How alerts work

An alert is a heads-up, not a trade. We tell you when the rate touches your target. Nothing is bought, sold or converted on your behalf, and the rate can move again before you act on it.
Rates can change between the alert and your confirmation. The rate that counts is the one shown when you confirm.
