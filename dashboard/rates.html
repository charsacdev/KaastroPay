How the rate is set

We take the mid-market price from three independent feeds and apply one published spread. The deposit rate sits just under mid; the withdrawal rate just above it. That gap is how we make money, and it is the same for everyone in .

Rates refresh every 30 seconds. The rate on your receipt is the one that was applied, not a later reconstruction.
