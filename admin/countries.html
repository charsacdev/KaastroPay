Why the rails differ

A dedicated bank account tied to a BVN is a Nigerian construct. It has no equivalent in Ghana, Kenya, Tanzania or Uganda, where the realistic funding rail is mobile money.

The same is true of identity: NIN and BVN in Nigeria, the Ghana Card in Ghana, NIDA in Tanzania. The verification flow is one page with five behaviours, driven entirely by this table.
